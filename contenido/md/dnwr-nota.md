# Downward nominal wage rigidity

*Example note — replace with your own text.*

Wages adjust upward freely but face a floor on cuts:

$$ W_t \geq \gamma\, W_{t-1}, \qquad \gamma \le 1 $$

When the constraint binds, a monetary contraction lowers employment more than an equal-size expansion raises it. This asymmetry is the core mechanism of the paper.
